FUNDAMENTAL LAWS

5 ITEMS
7

BOOKS ARE FOR USE

Definition: The primary purpose of a library is to make its books/documents available for use.
Example: Library arrangements, shelving and access should facilitate the actual use of books, rather than merely preserving them as objects that are difficult for readers to reach.
8

EVERY READER HIS BOOK

Definition: Every reader should be provided with the document or reading material appropriate to his or her needs and interests.
Example: A student's information need should be met by providing books relevant to that student's subject and level of study.
9

EVERY BOOK ITS READER

Definition: Every book or document has some potential reader for whom it is useful, and the library should facilitate its reaching that reader.
Example: A specialized scientific book may have only a small audience, but classification and arrangement should enable that audience to discover and use it.
10

SAVE THE TIME OF THE READER

Definition: Library organization and services should minimize the time and effort required by the reader to find and obtain the needed document or information.
Example: A logical classification arrangement enables a reader to locate books on a subject without unnecessary searching.
11

LIBRARY IS A GROWING ORGANISM

Definition: A library is a growing and changing organism; therefore its collections, organization, services and methods must be capable of growth and adaptation.
Example: A classification scheme must accommodate new subjects as new areas of knowledge arise.
